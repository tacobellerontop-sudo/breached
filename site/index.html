Light by leaving things out.

Breached is its own small interface on top of Mozilla's signed Firefox engine. Pages work like they do in Firefox. Everything around them is new and much smaller.

⌁

Keyboard first

Press : and type. :open, :close, :reader, :pin, :private. Tab completes, Up and Down recall history. A mouse still works too.

◐

Tabs that sleep

Tabs you haven't touched for 15 minutes let go of their memory and reload when you come back. Tabs playing sound or using your camera are left alone.

⛨

Nothing phoning home

No telemetry, no studies, no sponsored tiles, no Pocket, no account nagging. Site permissions are asked for right in the command bar.

⧉

Real extensions

Firefox extensions install from addons.mozilla.org, including the full uBlock Origin. Their buttons and menus show up in Breached's bars.

▶

Streaming works

Runs on Mozilla's own signed engine, so Netflix, Disney+ and Spotify play once you add the Protected video mod. Breached offers it the first time a site needs it.

⇣

Updates itself

Checks GitHub once a day and updates in place with :update. Your profile, settings and mods stay put. Prefer a zip? There's a portable copy too.

Your hands stay on the keys.

The tab bar is a thin strip, top, bottom or down the side, and can hide until you need it. The command bar appears only when you press :. Add the Vim keys mod for link hints with f and scrolling with j and k.

This page listens too. Press : and try download, mods, colour nord or help, or use j and k to scroll.

Make it yours in one turn of a dial.

The welcome page on first start lets you pick a colour set or spin a dial to build one around any colour, choose where the tab bar goes, and tick the mods you want. Every colour can be changed later in settings.

You may be wondering…

Is Breached really free?

Yes. No account, no subscription, no ads, nothing to sign in to. The code is on GitHub.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

Breached isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't know the publisher. Choose More info, then Run anyway. The installer and zip come straight from the project's GitHub releases.

Is it Firefox?

It runs on Mozilla's own signed Firefox engine, so pages work the way they do in Firefox. Everything around the page (tabs, command bar, settings, history, downloads) is Breached's own and much smaller. Firefox's telemetry, studies, Pocket and sponsored content aren't there.

Does uBlock Origin work?

Yes, the full version. Firefox extensions install from addons.mozilla.org, and their buttons and menus show up in Breached's bars. :ext ublock opens its popup.

Do Netflix and Spotify play?

Yes, with the Protected video mod. Breached offers it the first time a site needs it, and downloads Widevine from Mozilla when you say yes.

Where are bookmarks and saved passwords?

They're mods, so you only carry them if you use them: Bookmarks, Password manager, Bitwarden (with passkeys) and Firefox Sync. Tick them on the welcome page or add them later from the mods site.

How do I update?

Breached checks GitHub once a day. Open settings > Updates or type :update, and it installs the new version and starts again. Your profile and mods are kept.

Where can I report a bug?

Open an issue on GitHub.
