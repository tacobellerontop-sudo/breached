What's new in 0.1.5: everyday browser features that cost next to nothing in speed or memory.

- **Private windows.** `Ctrl+Shift+P`, `:private [address]` or `:open -p <address>` opens one. It keeps no history, and its cookies, cache and site data are thrown away when it closes. It saves no session, remembers no site permissions, and keeps its downloads off the normal list. A lavender line and a "private" mark show which window is which. Closing its last tab closes it (`Ctrl+Shift+W` closes any window).
- **A right-click menu.**
  - On a link: open it in a new tab or a private window, copy it, or save it.
  - On an image or video: open it, copy its address, or save it.
  - On selected text: copy it, or search for it.
  - In a text field: undo, cut, copy, paste, delete, and select all.
  - Anywhere else on the page: back, forward, reload, save the page, print, view the source, or copy the page's address.
  - Extensions' menu items show too, such as uBlock Origin's "Block element".
  - Sites with their own right-click menu keep it. Shift+right-click shows Breached's menu anyway.
- **Printing** with Windows' print dialog. Use `Ctrl+P` or `:print`, or a page's own print button.
- **Save page** (`Ctrl+S`, `:save`). This saves the page to your downloads folder.
- **View source** (`Ctrl+U`, `:view-source`).
- **Copy the address** (`:yank`).
- **Zoom is kept per site.** Zooming in on one page zooms the rest of that site, now and next time, and other sites keep their own zoom. `:zoom 100` resets a site.

Installed copies can update from settings > Updates (or `:update`).
