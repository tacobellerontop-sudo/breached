What's new in 0.1.2:

- **Site permissions.** When a site wants your camera, microphone, screen, location, notifications or the like, Breached asks in the command bar: allow, always, block or never. "Always" and "never" are remembered; see and forget them in settings > Privacy > site permissions (or `:permissions`), where you can also block camera, microphone, location or notifications without being asked. A tab using your camera, microphone or screen shows a red dot and is never unloaded.
- **Pinned sites** on the new tab page. Pin with "+ pin a site", the "pin" button next to a most-visited site, or `:pin` on any page. Unpin with the × or `:unpin`, and drag tiles to reorder them.
- **Passkeys from Bitwarden** (Bitwarden mod 1.2). When a site asks for a passkey, the ones in your Bitwarden vault show as rows to pick from, or, if you choose that in settings, the only one is used without asking. Login boxes that offer passkeys quietly get one from `:bw.exe`. When a site makes a new passkey you can keep it in Bitwarden. Pick "other" for Windows Hello or a security key.

Installed copies of 0.1.1 can update from settings > Updates.
