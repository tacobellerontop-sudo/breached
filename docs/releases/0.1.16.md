What's new in 0.1.16: the whole window, revamped.

- **A tab strip on top, like the sketch.** Tabs sit in the title bar's row, with a slanted left edge, an orange line on the selected tab, an X to close a tab, and a + button after the last tab for a new tab.
- **A real address bar.** Under the tab strip: back, forward and reload, then an address box. Type an address or a search and press Enter to open it in this tab, or Ctrl+Enter for a new tab. A lock shows only for https pages; http pages show an open lock. The ⋮ button at the end opens Settings. The `:` command bar is still there at the bottom for keyboard users, and Ctrl+L still opens it.
- **A dark new tab page.** The sketch's dark page: a centred search box with just an underline ("Search the web…"), a `/breached_` wordmark, and a thin glowing "breach" horizon line under the search. Pinned sites, most visited and the keyboard hints are still below.
- **Three small personal touches.** The new tab page says Good morning, afternoon or evening, and if any tabs have been put to sleep it says how many are asleep — Breached's lightness, made visible.
- **A new default colour set: Breach dark.** Dark bars and tabs with Breached's orange for highlights, replacing pastel silver and orange as the default. The pastel set is still on the settings page, under Appearance, along with Nord, Gruvbox and the rest. If you picked your own colours before, they stay as they were.

Installed copies update from settings > Updates (or `:update`).
