What's new in 0.1.10: bring your stuff over, and choose which tabs to reopen.

- **Import from Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi or Firefox.** Bookmarks (in a "From Chrome" folder and so on) and your recent history come over. Passwords come over from the .csv file those browsers export, since they keep their saved passwords locked. It's on the welcome page, and `:import` (or `:import passwords`) does it any time.
- **Ask which tabs to reopen.** Settings > Browsing, or the welcome page: when Breached starts it lists last time's tabs in the command bar, so you can reopen all, none or one. `:restore` lists them again later, whatever the setting.
- **The welcome page** has a new "your stuff" step for both of these, and offers the new Tab groups mod.
- Commands from mods that fail now say why in the command bar.

New on the mods site, for any Breached from 0.1.7: **Tab groups**, **Vim keys and link hints**, **Dark pages**, **Screenshots** and **Speed**.

Installed copies can update from settings > Updates (or `:update`).
