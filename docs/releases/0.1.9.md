What's new in 0.1.9: a welcome page, and back and forward buttons.

- **Welcome page** the first time Breached starts in a new profile. Pick a colour scheme, or turn the colour dial to make one around a colour you like (Shuffle for another, Dark for dark bars). Choose where the tab bar goes, and tick the mods you want; Breached installs them when you finish. Bring it back any time with `:welcome`. Existing profiles don't get it on update.
- **Back and forward buttons** at the start of the tab bar. They grey out when there's nowhere to go. Switch them off in settings > Tabs ("Back and forward buttons in the tab bar"). Alt+Left, Alt+Right, `:back` and `:forward` work either way.

Installed copies can update from settings > Updates (or `:update`).
