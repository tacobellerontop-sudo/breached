What's new in 0.1.11: Breached opens files.

- **PDFs open in Breached**, in Firefox's PDF viewer, whether you open one from a website or from your computer.
- **Breached is listed in Windows.** It shows under "Open with" for PDFs, web pages (.html), pictures (.svg, .webp, .avif) and text files, and in Settings > Apps > Default apps, where you can make it your browser or your PDF viewer. `:default-apps` opens that page.
- **Opening a file while Breached is running** opens it in a new tab. Before, Breached only came to the front.
- The "Firefox" entry Windows may still show under "Open with" is the engine inside Breached's folder and can't open files by itself. Pick Breached instead.

Installed copies can update from settings > Updates (or `:update`). The new file types are registered by the installer, so the portable zip doesn't add them.
