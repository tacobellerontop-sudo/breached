What's new in 0.1.15: passkeys inside Breached.

- **Passkeys no longer go to Windows.** With the Bitwarden mod (1.7), Breached answers every site's passkey request itself, inside the engine, using the passkeys in your Bitwarden vault. Windows Hello's passkey window doesn't open.
- **Unlocking Bitwarden loads its passkeys.** A site then signs in with its passkey straight away (if you have several accounts there, they show as rows). If the vault is locked when a site asks, Breached asks you to unlock it; then try the site's passkey button again.
- **New passkeys are kept in Bitwarden.** When a site makes a passkey, it's saved in your vault.
- **Login boxes that offer passkeys:** Ctrl+Shift+L shows the passkey first, then your logins.

Turning the mod's Passkeys setting off gives passkeys back to Windows Hello and security keys. Installed copies update from settings > Updates (or `:update`).
