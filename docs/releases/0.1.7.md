What's new in 0.1.7: mods now come from the Breached mods site, with four new ones.

- **The mods site:** https://tacobellerontop-sudo.github.io/breached-mods/
  - Open it in Breached (settings > Mods > Get mods, or `:mods get`) and press Install. Breached asks before it installs anything.
  - Settings > Mods now lists the mods you have, with their settings and a Remove button. Mods can also be added from a .zip or a folder.
- **New mods** for the bigger features Breached leaves out:
  - **Password manager.** Saves the passwords you type into sites, asked in the command bar, and fills them in next time. `:passwords` fills or copies one when a site has several.
  - **Bookmarks.** Ctrl+D (or `:bookmark`) bookmarks the page. Ctrl+B (or `:bookmarks`) lists them to open.
  - **Firefox Sync (experimental).** Sign in with your Mozilla account (`:sync in`) to sync history, bookmarks and passwords with Firefox. Some account pages may not work yet.
  - **Protected video (DRM)** is a mod now. The first time a site needs it, Breached offers "get the mod". That installs it, downloads Widevine and reloads the page. If you had it turned on in 0.1.6, Breached installs the mod for you.
- **Bitwarden** moved to the mods site too. If you had it installed, Breached downloads it again by itself.
- Without their mods, saved passwords and Mozilla accounts stay switched off, so Breached does less in the background.

Installed copies can update from settings > Updates (or `:update`).
