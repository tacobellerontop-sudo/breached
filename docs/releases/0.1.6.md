What's new in 0.1.6: streaming video, reader view, and more ways to place the tab bar.

- **Protected video (DRM)** for Netflix, Disney+, Spotify and similar sites.
  - It's off until a site needs it. The first time a site does, Breached asks once.
  - If you choose "turn on", Breached downloads Google's Widevine (about 10 MB) through Mozilla's update server and reloads the page.
  - Change it later in settings > privacy, or with `:drm on` and `:drm off`.
  - Streaming sites may limit the video quality they send to Firefox-based browsers, Breached included.
- **Reader view.** `:reader` (or F9) shows the page's article on its own: no ads, menus or sidebars. Run it again to go back. It's also in the right-click menu.
- **Tab bar on the left or right.** It shows as a column of tabs. Choose this in settings > appearance.
- **Show the hidden tab bar on hover.** With "Hide the tab bar" on, move the mouse to the window's edge where the bar sits, and it appears until the mouse moves away. This is on by default and can be switched off in settings > appearance.

Installed copies can update from settings > Updates (or `:update`).
