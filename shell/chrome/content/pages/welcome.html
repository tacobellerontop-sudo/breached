    passwords

    Other browsers lock their saved passwords, so export them to a .csv file first: in Chrome, Edge or Brave open Settings > Passwords (Password Manager) > Settings > Export passwords; in Firefox open about:logins, the ⋯ menu > Export passwords. Delete the file once it's in.

    mods
